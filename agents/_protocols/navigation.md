# Navigation: _protocols/

## What lives here?

**Persona-level protocols** loaded on reviewer and council dispatch. Buddy names them by path for the seat to read:
the persona, `reviewer-base.md` (the one reviewer protocol: checked scope, findings, output format, envelope) and, on
the Spec or UX Board, its board protocol (`spec-reviewer-protocol.md`, `ux-reviewer-protocol.md`).
`code-reviewer-base-extended.md` holds the conventions for writing a code-reviewer persona; seats do not load it.

Boundary against `skills/_protocols/`: those are skill-level mechanics (cross-loaded by skills). The protocols here are
persona-level (cross-loaded on persona dispatch).
